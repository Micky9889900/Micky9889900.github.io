Angry Donkey Fly: Slingshot — Privacy Policy

Developer: FrostCore Labs

Contact: safespot.crashreports@gmail.com

Effective date: November 16, 2026

App: Angry Donkey Fly: Slingshot (Android, package com.angrydonkey.fly)

This policy explains what information Angry Donkey Fly: Slingshot ("the app") handles, and how. The app is a slingshot physics puzzle game intended for users aged 13 and older.

1. NO ACCOUNTS, NO SERVERS OF OUR OWN

The app has no sign-in, no user accounts, no chat, no online multiplayer and no sharing features. Goblins and other enemies are computer-controlled (AI). We (FrostCore Labs) do not run our own servers for the game and do not receive your game data. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location.

2. INFORMATION STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

The app saves the following only on your device, in the app's private storage: coins, stars and best scores per level, unlocked and selected abilities and trails, ability power levels, sound, music, voices and haptics settings, whether help was seen, daily login streak, lucky-wheel and free-chest usage, free-coin reward usage, lifetime levels cleared, a session counter used for ad timing, and the last app-open time used for ad pacing.

The app turns off Android's automatic app-data backup, so this data is not copied to Android cloud backup by the app. It is not sent to us.

3. ADVERTISING (GOOGLE ADMOB)

The app is free and shows ads using Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent. There are no in-app purchases.

Ads appear as a banner on menu, world-map, level-select, shop and settings screens (never during gameplay), occasional full-screen ads when leaving the level-result screen (with cooldowns and first-level protections), optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch (for example, for an extra donkey, bonus coins, a free chest or lucky-wheel spins), an optional rewarded interstitial for triple coins after some wins, an app-open ad on cold start or return from background (with limits), and a native ad card only in reserved slots on the level-complete screen and the shop free-coins tab.

According to Google, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and UMP may automatically collect and share the following with Google, for advertising, ad measurement (analytics) and fraud prevention:

  • Device or other identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • App and ad interactions, such as app launches, ad views and taps;
  • Diagnostic and crash information about ad performance;
  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate (general) location.

This data is encrypted in transit. Ad content is limited to the "PG" rating or lower (not child-directed; 13+ audience).

4. CONSENT (EEA, UK AND SWITZERLAND)

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Google's consent message is shown before ads are requested, and personalization of ads depends on your choices. You can review or change your choices at any time with the "AD PRIVACY" button on the app's main menu or in Settings (shown when consent applies in your region). Settings also has a PRIVACY POLICY button that opens this page.

5. GOOGLE'S POLICIES AND YOUR AD SETTINGS

Google processes ad data under its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google ad settings: https://adssettings.google.com
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device's settings (for example Android Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your device).

6. PERMISSIONS

The app uses internet and network-state access to load ads. The advertising ID, ad-services and wake-lock permissions are added by the Google ads SDK. The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions. The game itself can be played without an internet connection; ads only load when you are online.

7. CHILDREN

The app is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 is using the app, please contact us at safespot.crashreports@gmail.com.

8. YOUR CHOICES AND DELETION

  • Uninstalling the app (or Android Settings > Apps > Angry Donkey Fly > Storage > Clear storage) deletes all data the app saved on your device.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings to break the link to past ad data.
  • Use the "AD PRIVACY" button in the app to review or change consent choices where applicable.
  • Data collected by Google is handled under Google's policies linked above. We do not hold it and cannot access or delete it for you.

9. CHANGES

We may update this policy when the app changes. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
