Aqua Pixel: Color by Number – Privacy Policy

Effective: November 5, 2026

Aqua Pixel: Color by Number ("the app") is published by FrostCore Games. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email, contacts, location, or photos. Your coloring progress, coins, and settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app. The app requests no dangerous permissions.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Google may collect and process device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model, and ad interaction data to show, measure, and limit ads and to prevent fraud. In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), the app asks for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy options. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

Saving and sharing

When you choose to save or share a finished picture, the image is written to your gallery or passed to the app you pick. We never receive it.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, it may offer to open your email app with a crash report. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email.

Children

The app is for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page.
