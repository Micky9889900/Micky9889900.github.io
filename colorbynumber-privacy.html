Aqua Pixel: Color by Number – Privacy Policy

Effective: November 5, 2026

Aqua Pixel: Color by Number ("the app") is published by FrostCore Games. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email, contacts, location, or photos. Your coloring progress, coins, and settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app. The app requests no dangerous permissions.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). All ads are child-directed, rated G, and non-personalized (see Children's Privacy below). Google may process limited device information, such as IP address and device model, to show, count, and limit ads and to prevent fraud. In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), the app asks for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy options. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

Saving and sharing

When you choose to save or share a finished picture, the image is written to your gallery or passed to the app you pick. We never receive it.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, it may offer to open your email app with a crash report. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email.

Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

Aqua Pixel is designed for children and families (ages 4+). We comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR rules for children (GDPR-K).

  • No personal information is collected. The app has no accounts, chat, or sign-in, and does not ask for names, emails, photos, contacts, or location. Progress stays on the device.
  • Child-directed ads only. Ads are served by Google AdMob with every request tagged as child-directed (tagForChildDirectedTreatment) and under the age of consent (tagForUnderAgeOfConsent), with a maximum ad content rating of G.
  • Non-personalized ads. Ads are never personalized or based on interests, and no behavioral profiles are built.
  • Advertising ID. The advertising ID is not used for personalization, profiling, or targeting. Google may process limited device data only as needed to show ads, count them, and prevent fraud, as allowed for child-directed apps.
  • Parental gate. Rewarded videos and all links that leave the app (privacy policy, rating, email) are behind a parental gate that a grown-up must answer.
  • Parents' rights. Because we do not collect personal information, we have nothing to review or delete. If you think a child has sent us information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.
