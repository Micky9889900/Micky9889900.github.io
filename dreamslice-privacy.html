Dream Slice — Privacy Policy

Developer: FrostCore Labs

Contact: safespot.crashreports@gmail.com

Effective date: November 8, 2026

App: Dream Slice (Android, package com.dreamslice.fruitfun)

This policy explains what information Dream Slice ("the app") handles, and how. Dream Slice is a fruit slicing game for players of all ages, including children.

1. NO ACCOUNTS, NO SERVERS OF OUR OWN

Dream Slice has no sign-in, no user accounts, no chat or social features, and no sharing features. We (FrostCore Labs) do not run our own servers for the game and do not receive your game data. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location.

2. INFORMATION STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

The app saves the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Game progress: stars per level, best scores, gems, unlocked and selected blades and friends, trophies, number of fruits sliced, best combo, daily gift date, and the sound setting.
  • Age group: the first time you open the app, it asks "How old are you?" with the options "Under 13", "13 to 15", "16 or older", or "Skip". Only the chosen group is saved on the device. We do not ask for or store a date of birth, and the answer is not sent to us.

The app turns off Android's automatic app-data backup, so this data is not copied to Android cloud backup by the app. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage (Android Settings > Apps > Dream Slice > Storage > Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app. This also resets the age question.

3. ADVERTISING (GOOGLE ADMOB)

Dream Slice is free and shows ads using Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent. The ad SDK is not started, and no ads are requested, until the age question has been answered (or skipped).

Ads appear as a banner on menu screens, occasional full-screen ads between rounds, and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch (for example, to retry a level with extra hearts).

According to Google, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share information such as your IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), interactions with ads and the app (such as app launches, taps and video views), diagnostic information, and device identifiers such as the app set ID, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.

How we limit this:

  • The app removes the Android advertising ID (AD_ID) permission, so the app does not provide access to the Android advertising ID.
  • "Under 13" and "Skip" (unknown age): ad requests are tagged for child-directed treatment, personalized (interest-based) ads and remarketing are disabled, and the ad content rating is limited to G (general audiences).
  • "13 to 15": ad requests are tagged as users under the age of consent and treated as teen, personalized ads are disabled, and ads are limited to G.
  • "16 or older": where required (for example in the EEA, UK or Switzerland), Google's consent message is shown before ads are requested, and personalization depends on your choices. Ads are limited to G. When required, an "Ad privacy" button on the main menu lets you review or change your choices.

These settings are signals to Google. Google processes ad data under its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID and manage ad settings in your device's Android settings.

4. PERMISSIONS

The app uses internet and network-state access to load ads. Wake-lock and foreground-service permissions are added by the Google ads SDK. The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions.

5. CHILDREN

We designed Dream Slice for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children ourselves. Children who choose "Under 13", and players who skip the age question, receive only non-personalized, child-directed ad treatment as described above. Parents or guardians with questions can contact us at safespot.crashreports@gmail.com.

6. YOUR CHOICES

  • Clear app storage or uninstall to delete all locally saved data.
  • Players aged 16 or older can review consent choices through the "Ad privacy" button when it is shown.
  • Data collected by Google is handled under Google's policies linked above. We cannot access or delete it for you.

7. CHANGES

We may update this policy when the app changes. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
