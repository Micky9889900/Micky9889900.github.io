Privacy Policy

App: Kohakutou Crystal Candy Shop (com.crystalcandystudio.maker)
Developer: Crystal Candy Studio
Last updated: November 22, 2026

This game is made for children. We care about their privacy and follow the Google Play Families Policy and children's privacy laws, including COPPA (USA) and GDPR (EU).

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for or collect any personal information. Children do not create accounts, and we never ask for a name, email, phone number, photo, or location.

Game progress (levels, coins, stars, and settings) is saved only on the device. It is never sent to us.

2. Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, a Google Play Families self-certified ad network. All ad requests are marked as child-directed and as coming from users under the age of consent. This means:

  • Only non-personalized ads are shown (no interest-based ads or remarketing).
  • Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences (G).
  • Ads are never shown during gameplay, only at natural breaks. Video ads that give rewards are always optional.

To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical information such as the device's advertising ID, IP address, device type, and app version, under child-directed limits. See How Google uses information from apps and the AdMob privacy information.

3. Consent (EU, UK, Switzerland)

Where required by law, the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for consent before ads are loaded.

4. Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. There are no chats, social features, external links for children to tap, or in-app purchases. If you believe information about a child was collected, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell or share any personal data. The only third party is Google AdMob, as described above.

6. Data deletion

All game data is stored on the device. Uninstalling the game deletes it completely.

7. Security

The game uses Google's secure (encrypted) connections for ads. No personal data is stored on our servers.
