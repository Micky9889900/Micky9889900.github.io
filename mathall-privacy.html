Privacy Policy for MathAll: All-in-One Calculator
Effective date: October 25, 2026

MathAll ("the app") is developed by FrostCore Labs ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles.

1. Information the app stores on your device
Your calculation history (up to 60 entries), settings (theme, DEG/RAD mode) and, if you choose to watch a rewarded ad, the time your 30-minute ad-free period ends are saved only on your device. They are never uploaded to me or anyone else. You can clear history inside the app at any time; uninstalling the app removes everything. If you use Android backup, these settings may be included in your own Google backup.

2. No accounts, no personal information
The app has no sign-in and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, files or precise location. I do not run any server and do not receive any data from the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): banner ads, occasional full-screen (interstitial) ads, sponsored "native" cards labelled "Ad" on the home screen and in the calculator list, and an optional rewarded video ad that you start yourself in exchange for 30 minutes without ads. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:
- device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID,
- approximate location derived from your IP address,
- app interaction data (for example ad views and clicks), and
- diagnostic and performance information.
This data is sent encrypted to Google and handled under Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services": https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Consent (Google User Messaging Platform)
In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and other regions where it is required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from "Privacy options" in the app's menu on the home screen. Where you do not consent, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Google > Ads).

5. Children
The app is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Permissions
INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE are used only to load ads and the consent form. The advertising ID permission is added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

7. Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new effective date.
